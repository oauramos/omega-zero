omega-zero's screen looks (SELECT+X in a game) are, but for the scanlines and the CRT, slot's ports
of shaders from the libretro shader collection
https://github.com/libretro/glsl-shaders
by way of slot, https://github.com/BrandonKowalski/slot

LCD by Gigaherz (LCD3x). Public domain.

Dot by Themaister. Public domain.
Changes in slot: sharper dots (falloff 4.0) and a 0.85 blend.

Grid, the line weighting of zfast_lcd_standard by Greg Hogan (SoltanGris42), copyright (C) 2017.

The scanlines and the CRT are omega-zero's own.
