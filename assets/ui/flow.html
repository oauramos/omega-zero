omega-zero flow

Every new screen of omega-zero's menus, built from the kit in storybook.html, and the slides between them as the device draws them: 280 ms, smootherstep, every frame on whole art pixels. Walk it with the keys or the buttons under the screen. Going deeper slides the screen out to the right and the next in from the left; going back is the mirror. Settings stands beside the shelf rather than under it, so the slides between the two go the other way round: the shelf out to the left and Settings in from the right, and back again Settings out to the right and the shelf in from the left. The screens under Settings, and Controls, slide the usual way. The backdrop behind them never moves with them, and the bottom bar never leaves, but for the screen after the power menu's choice, where nothing can be pressed and it sinks away. On the shelf the bar holds the shelf's system in a pill between its actions: L1 and R1 change it and it rises in with the new one, and a letter jumped to with Up or Down shows in it for a while.

The URL

The hash picks what is shown, so a still of any screen, or of any moment of a slide, can be taken with headless Chrome. The page keeps it up to date as you walk.
